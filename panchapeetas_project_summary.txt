================================================================================
                    VEERASHAIVA PANCHA PEETHAS PLATFORM
                Project Status, Accomplishments & Future Vision
================================================================================

This document provides a non-technical summary of the features and capabilities 
implemented on the platform to date, followed by a roadmap of planned future 
enhancements. 

Designed for devotees and temple administrators, the platform makes it easy to 
connect with the five sacred seats of the Veerashaiva tradition (Rambhapuri, 
Ujjaini, Kedara, Srisaila, and Kashi).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1: ACCOMPLISHMENTS (WHAT IS COMPLETED & WORKING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DIVINE HERITAGE PORTAL (HOMEPAGE & PEETHA PAGES)
   * Beautiful User Interface: A premium, modern web design that respects and 
     reflects the sacred nature of the Pancha Peethas.
   * Multi-Language Support: Devotees can read the site and book services in 
     seven languages: English, Kannada, Marathi, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and Malayalam.
   * Dedicated Peetha Pages: Each of the five Peethas has a dedicated page showcasing 
     its founding history, the current Swamiji, location details, and active sevas.

2. DEDICATED DEVOTEE PROFILES (PERSONAL SPIRITUAL SPACE)
   * Devotee Accounts: Secure signup and one-click login using a Google account.
   * Interactive Profile Details: Devotees can save their contact information 
     (phone, email, residential address) and spiritual coordinates (Gotra, 
     Nakshatra, Rashi).
   * Smart Photo Management: Devotees can easily upload, preview, or remove their 
     profile picture. When a photo is uploaded, it is displayed in a larger, 
     beautiful circular frame with a quick-edit option.
   * Visual Completion Progress: A circular progress bar rings the devotee's 
     avatar icon in the header, showing them how complete their profile is 
     (e.g., "88% Complete") to encourage them to finish entering their details.

3. DYNAMIC POOJA & SEVA BOOKING SYSTEM (DASHBOARD FOR DEVOTEES)
   * Smart Booking Autofill: When booking a pooja, the form automatically fills 
     in the devotee's name, phone, email, and spiritual details directly from 
     their profile. This saves time and avoids spelling errors in sacred names.
   * Color-Coded Slot Calendar: Instead of guessing dates, devotees use a visual 
     calendar that highlights availability in real-time:
     - Green: Slots are fully available.
     - Orange: "Booking Fast" (fewer than 2 slots remaining).
     - Red: Fully booked (blocking further selections).
     - Gray: Closed/past dates.
   * Multi-Member Family Bookings: Devotees can dynamically add family members 
     to a single booking and enter their details (Name, Gotra, Nakshatra, Rashi). 
     This ensures that the entire family's names are included in the daily temple 
     prayers (Sankalpa).
   * Secure Payments Integration: Configured Razorpay payment configurations for 
     each temple to support secure, online credit/debit card checkouts.

4. TEMPLE ADMINISTRATOR DASHBOARD (FOR PEETHA HANDLERS)
   * Dedicated Handler Portals: Specialized dashboards for managers of each 
     mutt to view bookings, see devotee counts, and download daily reports.
   * Pooja & Seva Management: Handlers can add, edit, or remove the poojas 
     offered by their Mutts. They can customize the price, set daily slot limits, 
     and specify which weekdays the pooja is conducted.
   * Live Streaming: Mutts can embed their live YouTube streams so that 
     devotees can watch daily prayers and special events live from home.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: FUTURE ENHANCEMENTS (WHAT IS COMING NEXT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. AUTOMATED MOBILE ALERTS (WHATSAPP & SMS)
   * Instant booking confirmations and e-receipts sent directly to the 
     devotee's mobile number.
   * Reminders a day before the booked pooja containing live stream links.

2. UPI & DIVERSIFIED PAYMENTS
   * Support for one-click mobile UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) 
     for a frictionless checkout experience.

3. PRASADAM COURIER TRACKING
   * If a seva includes sending sacred prasadam (blessed offerings) to the 
     devotee's home, the platform will generate tracking links so they can monitor 
     its transit and delivery status in real-time.

4. OFFLINE BOOKING SYNCHRONIZATION
   * A feature allowing mutt staff to record cash/card bookings made directly 
     at the physical temple counter. These will immediately update the online 
     calendar to prevent double-booking.

5. MEDIA GALLERY & ANNOUNCEMENTS
   * A devotional space featuring mutt photo galleries, news, event calendars, 
     spiritual quotes, and recorded video archives.
   * Option to subscribe to newsletter updates from the holy seats.
